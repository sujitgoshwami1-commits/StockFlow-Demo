HOW TO START
==================================================
1. Download the StockFlow ZIP package.
2. Extract the ZIP file.
3. Open the extracted folder.
4. Find:
   index.html
5. Double-click index.html.
6. StockFlow will open in your web browser.

==================================================
MAIN SECTIONS
==================================================
StockFlow includes the following main sections:
1. Dashboard
2. Products
3. Stock Movements
4. Suppliers
5. Categories
6. Reports

==================================================
1. DASHBOARD
==================================================
The Dashboard provides an overview of your inventory,
including low-stock alerts and a quick summary.

==================================================
2. PRODUCTS
==================================================
Use Products to manage your inventory items.

You can:
- Add a new product (SKU, name, category, supplier,
  buy price, sell price, stock, minimum threshold)
- Track current stock per product
- See which products are low on stock

==================================================
3. STOCK MOVEMENTS
==================================================
Use Stock Movements to record stock changes:
- Stock IN (new stock received)
- Stock OUT (stock sold or used)
- Adjustment (manual correction)

Each movement is logged with a note and timestamp.

==================================================
4. SUPPLIERS
==================================================
The Suppliers section is used to manage supplier
contact information, including phone, email and
address.

==================================================
5. CATEGORIES
==================================================
Use Categories to organize products into groups for
easier tracking and reporting.

==================================================
6. REPORTS
==================================================
The Reports section provides an inventory value
overview and low-stock reporting.

==================================================
DATA STORAGE
==================================================
The current browser-based version uses browser-based
local storage for application data.

Important:
- Data is stored locally in the browser.
- Data may not automatically synchronize between devices.
- Clearing browser data may remove stored application
  information.
- Regular backups are recommended.

==================================================
BACKUP
==================================================
Always maintain regular backups of important business
information.

Do not rely on a single browser or computer as the only
copy of important business data.

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================
This is the browser-based version of StockFlow.
It does not currently require PHP, MySQL or a server.

For a production business deployment, additional
features may be required, such as:
- Secure backend
- Database
- User authentication
- Barcode scanning
- Multi-location inventory
- Automated backups
- Cloud synchronization
